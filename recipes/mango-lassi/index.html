Mango Lassi

4.8/5 (0 reviews)

Thick, creamy and sunshine-sweet — the classic Indian mango lassi, blended in 5 minutes flat.

Step-by-Step Instructions

5 steps
  1. 1

    Step 1

    Peel and chop the mangoes, discarding the seed.

  2. 2

    Step 2

    Add mango, curd, milk, sugar, cardamom and ice cubes to a blender.

  3. 3

    Step 3

    Blend on high for 60-90 seconds until completely smooth and frothy.

  4. 4

    Step 4

    Taste and adjust sweetness or thickness — add milk to thin, more mango to thicken.

  5. 5

    Step 5

    Pour into tall glasses, garnish with pistachios if using, and serve immediately.

💡

Chef's Pro Tip

For optimal flavor, ensure all fresh spices are freshly roasted or ground before adding. Adjust seasoning gradually according to taste and let the dish rest for 3–5 minutes before serving.
